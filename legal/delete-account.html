Delete your account

You can delete your Yard Empire account here or in the game. You do not need the game installed to use this page.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, username and password.
  • Your saved game on our servers.
  • Your entry on the leaderboard, if you had one.

This happens straight away and cannot be undone. Routine backups kept by our hosting provider, where there are any, are replaced on their own schedule. A copy of the game saved on your own device stays there until you clear the game's data.

Delete it now

Your account and everything stored for it have been deleted.

Or in the game

Open Settings, then your account, then "Delete my account", and enter your password.

Forgot your password?

Set a new one first: in the game, open Settings, "Sign in or create account", "Sign in", then "Forgot password?". If you cannot do that, write to the address on the privacy policy page from the email of the account, and we will delete it for you.
