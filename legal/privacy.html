Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Yard Empire is a game about building yards and a company. This page says what the game keeps about you, why, and how to have it removed. The short version: you can play without an account and then nothing about you leaves your device. An account stores your email, a username and your saved game, so that you can play on any device.

Who is responsible

Yard Empire is made by Or Azoulay. For anything about your data, write to orazo123@gmail.com.

Playing without an account

Your saved game and your settings (sound, graphics and the like) are kept on your device only, in the game's own storage. They are not sent anywhere. "Reset progress" in the settings removes the saved game, and clearing the game's data in your browser or phone removes everything.

What an account stores

WhatWhy
Email addressTo sign you in and to send you a code when you forget your password. It is not shown to other players and not used for advertising.
UsernameThe name you choose. It is shown to other players only if you join the leaderboard.
PasswordStored only in a scrambled form that cannot be read back, by our sign-in provider.
Your saved gameSo that you can continue on another device: yards, upgrades, cash, company progress and records of play such as time played.
Leaderboard figuresOnly if you choose to join: your username, company value, number of yards and legacy stars are shown to other players. You can leave at any time.
A sign-in pass on your deviceSo that you stay signed in. It is kept in the game's own storage and removed when you sign out.
DatesWhen the account was made and when it last saved, to keep the service running and to spot misuse.

The game has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not read your contacts, your location, your photos or anything else on your device. It loads nothing from other sites while you play.

Where it is kept

Accounts and saved games are stored with Supabase, our hosting provider, on servers in the European Union. Like any internet service, their servers see the internet address a request comes from, and may keep it in logs for a limited time for security.

We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

How long it is kept

For as long as you have the account. When you delete the account, your email, username, saved game and leaderboard entry are removed from our systems together, straight away. Routine backups kept by our hosting provider, where there are any, are replaced on their own schedule.

Your choices

  • See your data: in the game, open Settings, then your account, then "Download my data".
  • Delete your account: in the game under Settings, your account, "Delete my account", or on the delete your account page.
  • Leave the leaderboard: in the Headquarters, on the Ranking tab.
  • Anything else: write to the address above.

Depending on where you live, the law may give you further rights over your data, such as correcting it or objecting to its use. Write to us and we will help.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly keep accounts of children under 13. If you believe a child has made an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
